# 05 – Implementation & Automation

## Overview

The final phase of the Quercis project focused on preparing the Business Intelligence solution for potential implementation at Carleon Europe BV.

After completing the business analysis, process improvements, data validation, and Power BI dashboard development, we explored automation opportunities and prepared the documentation needed to support implementation, maintenance, and cost evaluation.

## Implementation Activities

### 1. [Power Automate](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/Carleon-Europe-BV-accounts-receivable/blob/main/05%20%E2%80%93%20Implementation%20%26%20Automation/Carleon%20BV%20Power%20Automate%20Flow.jpeg)

We designed and tested a scheduled Power Automate workflow that retrieves KPI information from the Power BI dataset and sends automated email notifications when predefined conditions are met.

The workflow uses a scheduled trigger, queries the dataset, processes the results, and evaluates whether an email notification should be sent.

The screenshot demonstrates a successful execution of the workflow, including the automated email action.

This demonstrated how automated monitoring could reduce manual checking and help departments respond more efficiently to overdue payments.

### 2. [Implementation Plan](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/Carleon-Europe-BV-accounts-receivable/blob/main/05%20%E2%80%93%20Implementation%20%26%20Automation/Carleon%20BV%20Implementation%20Plan.docx)

We developed an implementation plan outlining the steps required to introduce the reporting solution into the organisation.

The plan considered implementation activities, stakeholder involvement, and the practical requirements for integrating the solution into existing operations.

### 3. [Service Level Agreement (SLA)](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/Carleon-Europe-BV-accounts-receivable/blob/main/05%20%E2%80%93%20Implementation%20%26%20Automation/sevice%20level%20AGREEMENT.pdf)

We prepared a Service Level Agreement defining the scope of the proposed solution, support responsibilities, service expectations, and implementation considerations.

This helped us understand the importance of maintaining and supporting a technical solution after development.

### 4. [Cost Estimation](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/Carleon-Europe-BV-accounts-receivable/blob/main/05%20%E2%80%93%20Implementation%20%26%20Automation/Carleon%20Cost%20Prediction.xlsx)

We developed a cost estimation to evaluate the financial considerations associated with implementing and maintaining the proposed solution.

This connected the technical development of the dashboard and automation workflow with business planning and feasibility.

## Outcome

As the final stage of the project, this phase brought together our technical solution and the business considerations required for its implementation.

Alongside the completed Power BI dashboard, we delivered an automated monitoring concept, implementation plan, Service Level Agreement (SLA), and cost estimation.

Together, these deliverables demonstrated how our proposed solution could support **more efficient Accounts Receivable monitoring, reduced manual work, and better-informed financial decisions**, while considering the practical requirements for introducing it into the organisation.
