# 04 – Power BI Dashboard

## Overview

This phase of the Carleon Europe BV project focused on developing an interactive Power BI dashboard to improve Accounts Receivable monitoring and support data-driven decision-making.

The goal was to visualise the financial KPIs identified during the analysis phase and provide the client with a clear overview of outstanding payments, overdue invoices, and customer account priorities.

## Dashboard Development

### 1. [Power BI Dashboard – My Development](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/Carleon-Europe-BV-accounts-receivable/blob/main/04%20%E2%80%93%20Power%20BI%20Dashboard/Power%20BI%20Dashboard%20-%20My%20Development.pbix)

I was responsible for the entire initial development of the Power BI dashboard. I started by investigating how to translate the KPIs we had previously identified and presented to the client into meaningful visualisations. This included determining which DAX formulas and calculations were needed, how the KPIs should be displayed, and which charts would best communicate the financial information.

Throughout the development process, I created multiple dashboard drafts, experimenting with different calculations, visualisations, and layouts. I continuously checked and improved the results to ensure that the financial information was displayed as accurately and clearly as possible.

The version included in this repository was my most accurate and complete development version before the final adjustments.

The dashboard focused on two main areas:

**Financial Performance Overview**
- Outstanding and overdue amounts
- Average overdue days
- Overdue rate
- Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)
- Financial trends over time

**Accounts Receivable Prioritisation**
- Customer accounts with outstanding balances
- Overdue invoices and payment delays
- Priority accounts requiring attention

During the later stages, a teammate and I also worked together to investigate relationships between columns in different tables. These connections were necessary to retrieve the correct data for certain calculations and visualisations.

Once I had completed the main dashboard, I identified the remaining issues and asked my teammate to make specific corrections before the final client presentation.

### 2. [Final Dashboard – Client Presentation](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/Carleon-Europe-BV-accounts-receivable/blob/main/04%20%E2%80%93%20Power%20BI%20Dashboard/POWERBI-CARLEON%20Final%20Dashboard.pdf)

After completing my development version and presenting the dashboard to the client, a few final improvements were needed.

One issue I identified was that certain customers were displayed using their IDs instead of their actual names. I asked my teammate to correct this so that the dashboard would display the customer names properly.

My teammate also updated the dashboard's colour scheme and improved its visual appearance to create a more consistent and professional final presentation.

These adjustments were made to the dashboard I had already developed, keeping the main structure, KPI calculations, and visualisations.

The PDF represents the final team deliverable presented to the client.

### 3. [Power BI Dashboard Documentation & Findings](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/Carleon-Europe-BV-accounts-receivable/blob/main/04%20%E2%80%93%20Power%20BI%20Dashboard/Power%20Bi%20Documentation.pdf)

This document provides a detailed explanation of the dashboard's development, KPI logic, design decisions, and analytical findings.

The document provides additional context behind the technical and business decisions made throughout the dashboard development process.

## Outcome

The completed Power BI dashboard provided a Business Intelligence solution for monitoring financial performance and prioritising overdue customer accounts.

My main contribution was **independently developing the dashboard through multiple iterations**, including researching and implementing DAX calculations, designing the visualisations, structuring the reporting pages, and validating the accuracy of the displayed information.

I also collaborated with a teammate on relationships between tables and coordinated the final corrections to customer name displays and the dashboard's colour scheme.

This project strengthened my skills in Power BI, DAX, data modelling, financial analysis, iterative development, and translating business requirements into a practical reporting solution.
