# 01 – Business Analysis

## Overview

The first phase of the Quercis project focused on understanding Carleon Europe BV's existing Accounts Receivable process and identifying inefficiencies in invoice monitoring and payment follow-ups.

As a team, we analysed the current workflow, investigated operational bottlenecks, and identified key stakeholders. Our findings provided the foundation for designing a more efficient process.

## Business Analysis Activities

### 1. [Fishbone Analysis](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/Carleon-Europe-BV-accounts-receivable/blob/main/01%20%E2%80%93%20Business%20Analysis/Fishbone%20Quercis.pdf)

We used a Fishbone Diagram to investigate the root causes of the identified problems, including manual processes, limited automation, and fragmented communication between departments.

### 2. [IST Process with Bottlenecks & Wastes](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/Carleon-Europe-BV-accounts-receivable/blob/main/01%20%E2%80%93%20Business%20Analysis/Quercis%20IST%20with%20Bottlenecks%20and%20Wastes.pdf)

We developed a BPMN model of the existing Accounts Receivable process, identifying bottlenecks and wastes such as manual invoice tracking, communication delays, and inefficient payment follow-ups.

### 3. [Stakeholder Analysis – Power vs Interest](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/Carleon-Europe-BV-accounts-receivable/blob/main/01%20%E2%80%93%20Business%20Analysis/Power%20vs%20Interest%20Carleon%20BV.png)

We analysed the influence and interests of key stakeholders, including the CFO, Finance Department, Customer Service, and clients, to understand their priorities and involvement in the proposed solution.

## Outcome

Our analysis revealed that the Accounts Receivable process relied heavily on manual Excel tracking, making it difficult to maintain an up-to-date overview of outstanding invoices. Delayed communication between Finance and Customer Service also slowed down payment follow-ups, while the lack of clear prioritisation made it harder to identify which overdue accounts required immediate attention.

These findings highlighted the need for centralised financial reporting, improved information sharing, and more efficient payment monitoring. They formed the foundation for the SOLL process, GAP analysis, and requirements developed in the next project phase.
