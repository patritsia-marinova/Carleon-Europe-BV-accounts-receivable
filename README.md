# Carleon BV – Accounts Receivable & Business Intelligence

## Project Overview

During Semester 3 of my ICT & Business studies at Fontys University of Applied Sciences, I worked on a group project for Carleon Europe BV to improve its Accounts Receivable process through business analysis, data analytics, and automation.

The organisation relied heavily on manual invoice tracking, Excel exports, and communication between departments to monitor outstanding payments. This made it difficult to identify overdue invoices, prioritise customer follow-ups, and maintain an accurate overview of financial performance.

Our goal was to develop a Business Intelligence solution that would improve financial visibility, reduce manual work, and support data-driven decision-making.

## From Business Problem to BI Solution

The project followed five main stages:

1. **Business Analysis:** Investigated the existing Accounts Receivable process (IST) through stakeholder interviews, BPMN modelling, bottleneck identification, and Fishbone Analysis.

2. **Process Improvement:** Designed an improved future-state process (SOLL), conducted a GAP analysis, defined requirements, and explored opportunities to improve invoice monitoring and communication.

3. **Data Modelling & KPI Development:** Developed a dimensional data model and used Python and Pandas to validate financial data and KPI calculations, including Days Sales Outstanding (DSO).

4. **Power BI Dashboard:** Developed an interactive dashboard, refined KPI calculations, investigated data quality issues, and improved the reporting solution through stakeholder feedback.

5. **Automation & Implementation:** Explored automated KPI notifications using Power Automate and prepared an implementation plan, Service Level Agreement (SLA), and cost estimation.

## The Power BI Dashboard

The main deliverable was a two-page Power BI dashboard designed for the Finance and Customer Service departments.

**Page 1 – Financial Performance Overview**

Provides insights into Total Overdue Amount, Total Outstanding Amount, Average Overdue Days, Overdue Rate, and DSO, supported by financial trends and interactive date filters.

**Page 2 – Accounts Receivable Prioritisation**

Helps identify customers requiring follow-up through priority rankings, overdue invoice details, outstanding balances, and payment delay analysis.

Throughout development, we improved the dashboard by validating calculations, refining the dimensional data model, and addressing duplicate invoice records that affected DSO reporting.
