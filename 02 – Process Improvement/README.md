# 02 – Process Improvement

## Overview

Following the business analysis, the second phase of the Quercis project focused on designing a more efficient Accounts Receivable process for Carleon Europe BV.

As a team, we explored how automation, improved information sharing, and centralised financial reporting could address the bottlenecks identified in the existing workflow.

## Process Improvement Activities

### 1. SOLL Process

We developed a future-state BPMN model illustrating how the Accounts Receivable process could be improved through automated invoice monitoring, clearer communication between departments, and more structured payment follow-ups.

### 2. GAP Analysis

We compared the existing IST process with the proposed SOLL process to identify the changes required.

The analysis highlighted opportunities to reduce manual Excel tracking, introduce automated monitoring, and improve how overdue invoices are identified and prioritised.

### 3. Requirements Analysis

I personally developed the project requirements, translating the identified business problems and stakeholder needs into clear expectations for the proposed solution.

These requirements helped guide the development of the Power BI dashboard and ensured that the solution addressed the organisation's operational challenges.

### 4. Dashboard Prototype

We created an initial dashboard prototype to explore how financial KPIs, outstanding balances, and overdue invoice information could be presented to Finance and Customer Service.

This provided a starting point for the dashboard's structure and functionality before its development in Power BI.

## Outcome

This phase established a clearer direction for improving the Accounts Receivable process. We proposed moving from fragmented Excel-based monitoring toward **centralised financial reporting, automated payment monitoring, and better prioritisation of overdue accounts**.

The SOLL process, GAP analysis, requirements, and prototype formed the foundation for the data modelling and Power BI development in the following phases.
